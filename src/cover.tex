\lettersection{About Me}
I believe data has the power to transform healthcare, not just by improving efficiency but by ensuring every patient receives the right care at the right time. Throughout my career in healthcare data engineering, I’ve seen how fractured systems and inconsistent data can stand in the way of better outcomes. That’s why I’ve committed myself to building pipelines and platforms that don’t just move data but make it meaningful. For me, healthcare analytics isn’t about dashboards and metrics—it’s about lives impacted, clinicians empowered, and health journeys made smoother.

Most recently, I designed an AWS data lake that significantly improved retrieval speeds and streamlined operations. By automating CI/CD, I helped cut deployment times in half, making data more accessible and reliable for the organization. While much of my recent work has been in healthcare, I have also contributed to other industries, giving me a versatile perspective on data engineering challenges.

\lettersection{Why Cohere Health?}
Cohere Health stands out because it starts with the same mission: a commitment to transforming the healthcare journey through intelligent, patient-centered solutions. Your approach to aligning payers, providers, and patients reflects a belief that collaboration——not confrontation——drives better outcomes. The way Cohere leverages AI and clinical expertise to streamline care paths resonates with my passion for turning raw data into actionable insights. I’m inspired by how you’re not just reducing friction in prior authorization but reshaping how healthcare is delivered and experienced.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
I bring over 15 years of experience in healthcare data curation, integration, and transformation. My work with AWS, dbt, and Spark has been driven by a commitment to ensuring data flows seamlessly from ingestion to insight. Whether building ETL pipelines for payer data, transforming FHIR and HL7 datasets into Parquet for efficient querying, or leading data validation efforts in Athena, my focus has always been on delivering reliable, actionable insights. I don’t just build pipelines—I build trust in the data that powers decisions. I’d be honored to bring that same commitment to Cohere Health’s mission.  

Thank you for considering my application. I’d love the opportunity to discuss how my experience can contribute to Cohere’s work in transforming healthcare.
