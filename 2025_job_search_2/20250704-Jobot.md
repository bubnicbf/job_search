## EMR Data Architect – Remote (Columbus, OH)

**Salary:** $220,000 – $275,000/year  
**Location:** Remote (HQ in Columbus, OH)  
**Hosted By:** Sydney Weaver  

### Summary
Join a fast-growing, Series B healthcare SaaS startup as an **EMR Data Architect**, a high-impact and visible role with a clear path to **Chief Data Architect**. You'll lead the data strategy as the platform expands beyond home health into other outpatient specialties.

### Responsibilities
- Design a scalable, specialty-agnostic clinical data model  
- Define and maintain the architecture blueprint (patients, visits, claims, payments)  
- Lead schema migration for multi-specialty support without disrupting production  
- Build ETL pipelines to standardize EMR data (HL7, FHIR, EDI)  
- Implement data quality and observability tools  
- Architect pipelines and schemas with HIPAA compliance baked in  
- Develop identity resolution systems to deduplicate records  
- Optimize SQL and relational design for performance at scale  
- Design real-time data pipelines (e.g., Kafka)

### Requirements
- Bachelor's in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or related field  
- 10–20 years in healthcare data systems (seniority-based)  
- Proven experience as a senior or chief data architect  
- Deep knowledge of EMR, billing, and claims data modeling  
- Strong in HL7, FHIR, EDI 837/835, ICD‑10, CPT  
- Experience with cross-specialty EMR platforms (Epic, Athenahealth, Abridge)  
- Expert in relational DB design, SQL tuning, and data deduplication  
- HIPAA-compliant engineering experience  
- Excellent communication and stakeholder alignment skills
